Support

Need help with Brick Rush? Email batu@bkverse.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

I bought something and did not get it. Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Still missing? Email us with the date of the purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game? Not if iCloud is on: your progress is backed up to your own iCloud account and comes back when you install the game again on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Settings › iCLOUD BACKUP in the game shows whether the backup is on.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up or login in Brick Rush.

How do I turn off ads? Buy "Remove Ads" in the shop. Optional reward ads stay available if you want them.
