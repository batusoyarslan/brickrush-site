Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Brick Rush: Wall Dash ("the game") is made by BKverse ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

No account, no personal profile

The game has no sign-up and we do not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, photos or location. You can pick an optional runner name in the game (a nickname of up to 12 characters); it is shown only on your own device: it is never sent to us, to Game Center or to advertisers, and other players never see it. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, unlocked characters and items, runner name, settings, best score) is saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud is turned on, backed up to your own iCloud account so it can come back on your other devices. We never receive this data. Deleting the game deletes the copy on your iPhone.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score and Game Center nickname are sent to Apple to show leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase happened so the game can deliver the item.

Advertising

The free version shows ads, including optional ads you can watch for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device and usage information such as your IP address, device model, operating system, ad interactions and, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised with that identifier. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads between runs.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off tracking at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, sign out of Game Center in Settings › Game Center, or delete the game (and its iCloud backup in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage) to remove all saved data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Questions: batu@bkverse.com.
